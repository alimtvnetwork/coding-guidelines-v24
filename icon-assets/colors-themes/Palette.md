## Usage

- `Primary` for interactive accents, links, and the logo braces.
- `PrimaryGlow` only as a gradient partner to `Primary` — never as body text on light backgrounds.
- `Foreground` on `Background` for light mode; `ForegroundDark` on `SurfaceDark` for dark mode.
